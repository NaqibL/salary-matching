# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-05-31*

## Product Overview
**Name:** Lowball
**One-liner:** Check if your Singapore salary offer is fair — instantly.
**What it does:** Pulls live job listings from MyCareersFuture (which has salary data on every listing), embeds them with BGE, and lets anyone benchmark their salary against real market data for similar roles.
**Product category:** Salary intelligence / job market analytics
**Product type:** Free web tool
**Business model:** Free, no signup required

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** N/A — individual job seekers
**Decision-makers:** Job seekers, fresh grads, people negotiating offers
**Primary use case:** Check whether a salary offer is fair before accepting or negotiating
**Jobs to be done:**
- Know if I'm being underpaid before I accept an offer
- See what similar roles pay in Singapore right now
- Understand market salary ranges without cold-emailing recruiters

**Use cases:**
- Paste a job title + description + offered salary → see where it ranks vs. similar listings
- Browse market salary dashboard by role / company / seniority

## Personas
| Persona | Cares about | Challenge | Value we promise |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| Fresh grad | Am I being low-balled? | No salary reference point | Real market data for exact role type |
| Mid-career switcher | What's market rate in new field? | Salary bands vary wildly | Semantic search finds truly similar roles |
| Technical job seeker | Transparent, data-driven signal | Glassdoor is stale/crowdsourced | Live MCF/CAG listings, updated daily |

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Singapore job seekers have no reliable, real-time, free salary benchmark — Glassdoor is crowdsourced and stale, MOM data is annual aggregates.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Glassdoor: user-submitted, sparse for SG, often outdated
- MOM salary data: annual, coarse-grained, no role-level detail
- LinkedIn Salary: paywalled, also crowdsourced
**What it costs them:** Accepting underpaid offers, weak negotiation position, wasted time researching
**Emotional tension:** "I don't know if I should push back on this offer or if I'm already getting a good deal"

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Glassdoor SG — falls short because data is user-submitted and sparse for Singapore
**Secondary:** MOM annual salary report — falls short because it's annual, coarse, no role-level granularity
**Indirect:** Asking friends / Reddit — falls short because it's slow and sample size is tiny

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Live data: crawls MCF + CAG daily, not crowdsourced
- Semantic matching: finds similar roles by meaning, not just keywords
- Free, no account needed
- Built specifically for Singapore market
**How we do it differently:** Semantic embedding (BGE) matches your role description to real active listings
**Why that's better:** Keyword search misses roles with different titles; semantic search finds what's actually comparable
**Why customers choose us:** Only free, real-time, Singapore-specific salary benchmark

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** Direct, data-driven, no fluff
**Style:** Technical but accessible — numbers and specifics over vague claims
**Personality:** Built by a dev for job seekers, not a VC-backed HR platform

## Goals
**Business goal:** Drive awareness, organic traffic, and word-of-mouth among SG tech/professional job seekers
**Conversion action:** Use the salary checker → share it
