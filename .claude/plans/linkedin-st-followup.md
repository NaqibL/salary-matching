Since its release, Lowball has seen quite a few improvements:

- Hot Jobs : A new page that flags roles on MCF paying well above the median for that role, updated daily.

- Fine-tuned matching model : Retrained on 240k+ real Singapore job listings, so matches should feel noticeably more relevant now.

- UI/UX improvements and bug fixes : Smoothed out rough edges from launch and fixed issues people flagged.
